Question 5 – Writing (Sentence Completion)
Choose the sentence that is grammatically correct and best combines the ideas.
Maria studies hard. She wants to get a scholarship.
A) Maria studies hard so that she can get a scholarship.
B) Maria studies hard because she can get a scholarship.
C) Maria studies hard although she wants a scholarship.
D) Maria studies hard, so she wants a scholarship.

1. B
2. B
3. B
4. C
5. A
